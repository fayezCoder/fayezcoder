C130 Aircraft Management

A specialized tool for managing aircraft issues and tracking maintenance operations.

The C130 Aircraft Management System is designed to streamline aircraft maintenance and issue tracking for specialists and commanders. The app allows:

  • Specialists to create and manage issues, including aircraft number, issue name, description, estimated time, corrective actions, and status (Open, In Progress, Resolved).
  • Image attachments for detailed reporting.
  • Role-based access, where over 10 different specialties can log issues without visibility into others' records.
  • A Commander role with full access to track all specialties' issues, set priorities (High, Medium, Low), and send messages or orders to specialists.

This comprehensive system enhances maintenance efficiency, improves communication between specialists and commanders, and ensures smooth aircraft management operations.

AeroSupervisor

A high-performance workstation app for aviation supervisors, mechanics, and technicians.

AeroSupervisor is a high-performance workstation application built specifically for Aviation Supervisors, Line Mechanics, and Flight Line Technicians. Designed for hangar and flight line operations, AeroSupervisor brings technical knowledge, maintenance logs, and engineering tracking into one organized, offline workstation.

Whether you're performing routine line maintenance, referencing ATA chapters, or tracking active Engineering Orders (E.O.s), AeroSupervisor streamlines your shift and keeps your operational data organized.

  • Operations dashboard with active E.O.s, maintenance processes, knowledge cards, and gallery attachments
  • Technical knowledge base with ATA-linked knowledge cards and reference schematics
  • Maintenance and engineering tracking with photographic evidence and completion statuses
  • Interactive image viewer with zoom, pan, and gallery support
  • Offline and secure backup with local NoSQL storage and ZIP export/restore

Designed and developed by Fayez Almutairi for aviation mechanics and supervisors.
